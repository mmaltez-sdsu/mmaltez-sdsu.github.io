# Michael Maltez's Data Science Page

## My Selected Data Set: Movebank
**[Movebank](https://www.movebank.org/cms/movebank-main)**

Movebank is a free online database of animal tracking data, hosted by the Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior. Researchers around the world use it to store and share GPS and satellite tracking data for thousands of animals, including birds, mammals, and marine species.

**Potential applications:**
- Studying how ocean warming, such as a strong El Niño event, shifts the movement and migration routes of marine animals like whale sharks
- Identifying important feeding and breeding areas to support conservation and marine protected areas
- Predicting where animals will be in the future by combining tracking data with ocean temperature data

## My Selected Data Science Book: A Course in Machine Learning
**[A Course in Machine Learning](http://ciml.info/)** by [Hal Daumé III](http://hal3.name/), University of Maryland

I chose this book because it introduces machine learning in a clear, beginner-friendly way. Beyond my interests in the outdoors and understanding how the global heating impacts life on Earth, I have a deep interest is neuroscience, especially sleep research. The field has decades of data from EEG, MRI, and MEG studies, but it has struggled to unify these data sets. I want to learn how machine learning can help synthesize data like this and find patterns that no single study could reveal on its own.
